FoxHole native transaction utility, ABI 1

The separate transaction executable is covered by GPL-2.0-only. Mbed TLS 3.6.6 and its framework are used under their GPL-2.0-or-later version 2 option; original notices are retained in MbedTLS-GPL2.txt. Zlib 1.3.1 retains its original license. The executable dynamically uses the OpenWrt musl and libgcc packages; their libraries are not copied into this package.
